What We Do

Five reasons to come in.

Made cold.
By hand.
Every morning.

We started with one freezer and a single conviction — that gelato made from real ingredients, in small batches, tastes completely different. It still does. Single-origin chocolate. Madagascan vanilla. Strawberries pressed the same morning they are served.

Small batch · Hand-spun daily · Single-origin

The drip.
The stack.
The scoop.

Biscoff pancakes. Chocolate-drip waffles. Freakshakes tall enough to photograph. Everything on the dessert menu is made to be the best part of your day — and we take that seriously.

Waffles · Pancakes · Freakshakes

Quiet mornings.
Good coffee.
Nothing complicated.

Cappuccinos pulled properly. Americanos long and clean. Espresso the way it is supposed to taste. We do not rush the coffee, and neither should you.

Espresso · Cappuccino · Cold brew

Breakfast
to dinner.
Made with care.

Lebanese labneh sandwiches at 9am. Beef burgers at lunch. Spaghetti and meatballs when the afternoon stretches long. The menu is short because everything on it is made right.

Breakfast · Lunch · Dinner

Your next event.
Our table.

Birthday dinners. Corporate lunches. Baby showers with gelato instead of cake — because why not. We host private and group events at Frozen Spoon, and we put the same care into the experience as we do into everything on the menu.
